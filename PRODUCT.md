# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Indonesian office workers, consultants and students who sit in many online meetings (Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams), often in mixed Indonesian and English, sometimes Javanese. Most found Hush through a Threads post and are not technical. They want notes they do not have to write, and a safety net when someone suddenly asks them a question. The landing page is where they decide to sign up for the waitlist or install.

## Product Purpose
Hush is a free, open-source macOS app that records a meeting from the Mac itself (no bot joins the call), transcribes it on the Mac, and writes the summary, decisions and tasks. During the call, a small prompter under the notch suggests an answer when someone asks the user a question. Success for the site: visitors understand this in one screen, trust that their audio stays on their Mac, and leave their email (Mac users can install right away).

## Brand Personality
Quiet, private, attentive. "Tenang & privat": calm confidence, never hype. Hush is the friend sitting beside you who writes everything down and whispers the answer when you blank. Warmth comes from small human touches (an illustration, a playful moment), not from loud color. References: Granola (calm, editorial, the product shown simply) and Wispr Flow (illustration and personality), without copying either.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS: gradients, identical card grids, big stat numbers, "AI-powered" hype.
- Anything that resembles Wispr Flow Notetaker's look.
- Cream/beige "AI editorial" pages (paper background plus elegant serif as the whole identity).
- Techy/hacker: terminals, code-first, neon on black. It scares away non-technical users.

## Design Principles
1. Show the real product doing its job. Real screenshots and real moments from a call, never fake UI.
2. Quiet, not empty. Calm surfaces with one deliberate moment of delight per section.
3. Honest about limits. Mac only, Apple Silicon, answers can be wrong, text goes to the AI you pick. Trust is the product.
4. Speak like a person on Threads. Plain Indonesian, short sentences, no corporate tone.
5. Privacy is the story. "Your audio stays on your Mac" carries the page, so the page feels like night: quiet and private.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA contrast for all text and controls. Every animation has a reduced-motion alternative, and content is visible without JavaScript. Keyboard reachable form with visible focus. Indonesian first; works at 360 px wide phones.
